About

Rob Soukup is a Design Engineer in professional audiovisual and unified-communications integration. Since 2015 he has worked at AVI-SPL, first as a lead field commissioning engineer on large-scale corporate integrations and, since 2021, as a project engineer specializing in AutoCAD drawings and procurement.

Before that, from 2008 to 2015, he was Director of Media Services at The Orchard in Arlington Heights, IL, where he launched three campuses’ audiovisual systems and designed classroom and meeting-room systems.
